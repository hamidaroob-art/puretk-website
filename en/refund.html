1. Nature of the service

Si Park fees are calculated after a session ends, based on the actual recorded time. Fees for time actually parked are therefore non-refundable, except in the cases listed below.

2. When you are entitled to a refund

  • Duplicate charge for the same session.
  • Plate misread resulting in a charge for a session that is not your vehicle's.
  • Incorrect duration caused by a technical fault — the difference is refunded.
  • Bay unusable after entry for a reason attributable to the Company or the site, such as an unannounced closure.
  • Charge after vehicle removal, where you removed the vehicle from your account before the session.

3. Non-refundable

  • Fees for time actually used, including an hour that began but did not finish.
  • Published overstay fees for remaining after the availability window ends.
  • Charges from a vehicle not removed from your account after it was sold or handed over.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp +966 54 244 4477 or by email at info@puretk.sa.
  2. Include the receipt number, plate number, session date and the reason.
  3. Submit your request within 30 days of the charge.

5. Review and decision

We acknowledge your request within one business day, review it against the camera and session records, and inform you of the decision within 7 business days.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved amounts are returned to the same payment method used for the charge, and usually appear within 7–14 business days depending on your card issuer. You receive a credit note for the refunded amount. Refunds are not paid in cash.

7. Appeals

If you disagree with the decision, you may request a re-review within 14 days of being notified, without prejudice to your right to approach the competent consumer-protection authorities.
