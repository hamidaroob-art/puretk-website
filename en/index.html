Our specialty: forward deployed engineering

Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

An engineer embedded with your team on site who learns how work really happens, then builds, customises and integrates the AI solution until it runs in production with measurable impact.

  1. Embed

    We work alongside your team on site and see how the work is really done.

  2. Build

    We design the AI solution and tailor it to your data and processes.

  3. Deploy

    We integrate it with your existing systems and run it in the real environment.

  4. Measure

    We track impact against agreed numbers and refine until it is met.

SI ParkSuper Intelligent Parking

Parking that knows you.

SI Park opens private enclosed parking in residential and commercial buildings and malls, and organisations' car parks outside their working hours, to drivers with no attendant at the gate.

  1. 1

    Register once

    Scan the QR code at the gate and add your name, vehicle and payment card. Once is enough for every SI Park site.

  2. 2

    Drive straight in

    The camera reads your plate on entry and you receive a welcome message with the date and time.

  3. 3

    Leave and it's charged

    Your actual parking time is charged to your card automatically; the receipt arrives as you exit.
