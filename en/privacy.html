1. Who we are

Pure Technologies Company, a single-person limited liability company (Commercial Registration No. 7055160639), is the controller of your personal data in the Si Park service and this website, under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and its regulations.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, mobile number, email (optional), preferred language.
  • Vehicle data: plate number, plate country and vehicle type.
  • Usage data: plate and vehicle images on entry and exit, interior camera snapshots of the car park, entry and exit times, and the site used.
  • Payment data: processed by a licensed payment gateway; we keep only a reference token and the card's last four digits, never the full card number.
  • Employee programmes: the organisation code and employee number when registering through one.
  • Website data: basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide the Service: recognising vehicles, timing sessions, billing and issuing receipts.
  • To send you welcome messages, receipts and alerts about your sessions and account.
  • Security, documentation, fraud and payment-evasion prevention, and handling claims and damage.
  • Compliance with law and official requests.
  • Improving the Service and system accuracy using aggregated or de-identified data.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, to perform our contract with you, to meet a legal obligation, or for a legitimate interest that does not override your rights — such as protecting sites and documenting rights.

5. Sharing

  • Site owners see only revenue and occupancy totals — never plates, vehicle images or driver details.
  • Service providers: payment gateways, SMS providers and cloud hosting providers, only as needed and under contractual data-protection obligations.
  • Insurers and affected parties: when damage occurs, as needed to establish rights.
  • Authorities: upon a lawful request.

We never sell your personal data.

6. International transfers

Some data may be hosted by cloud providers outside the Kingdom, only in line with the regulatory controls on cross-border transfer of personal data and with appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

  • Session images and camera snapshots: 90 days from the session, unless subject to a claim, dispute or official request, in which case they are kept until it is resolved.
  • Financial records and receipts: for the period required by law.
  • Account data: for the life of the account, then deleted or de-identified unless the law requires retention.

8. Your rights

Under the law you have the right to be informed about how your data is processed, to access it and obtain a copy, to request its correction or completion, to request its destruction when no longer needed, and to withdraw consent. We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and administrative controls, including encryption in transit and at rest, restricted access and access logging. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Cookies

This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Its font is loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when it does.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the updated version is published here with its date, and we will notify you of material changes.
